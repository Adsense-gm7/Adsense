⚡ Quick Answer

Chapter 04 · Quick Quiz

What do you think?

Make your guess first. 84% of people get at least one wrong.

🎨
Does your car's color affect your premium?
❌ MYTH — Color never matters
Color is never factored into insurance pricing. Red cars don't cost more — that's a myth since the 1950s.
But here's the twist → WHERE you park at night matters. High-theft ZIP codes = +23% premium.
🚗
Safer cars = cheaper insurance?
❌ NOT ALWAYS
Volvo = safest car on earth. Also one of the most expensive to repair. Its insurance can be higher than a "less safe" car.
Companies actually care about → Repair cost + theft rate + rollover risk. Not just safety stars.
💳
Does your credit score affect your premium?
✅ TRUE — In 46 states
Poor credit = up to 76% more for the same coverage. Credit score predicts claims better than driving history.
Only CA, HI & MA ban this → In those states, your credit score cannot legally affect your rate.
📍
Moving 2 miles can change your rate by $4,000/year?
✅ YES — Dramatically
In Detroit, some ZIP codes are $4,000/year more expensive than neighboring ZIP codes just 3 miles away. The same driver, same car, totally different price.
Why? → Accident density, theft reports, hospital costs, and lawsuit frequency are all ZIP-specific. You're not just paying for your risk — you're paying for your neighborhood's risk.
